## 2. Which apps focus on grammar specifically?

### Takeaway
The big-revenue Korean adult English apps (스픽, 말해보카, 야나두, 케이크, Duolingo) are speaking, vocab, or general-purpose subscriptions priced around 100,000 to 300,000원 a year. Dedicated grammar apps are either publisher companions (해커스 그래머 게이트웨이) or small indie apps (영문법왕국, English Star, AI 문법 앱) with little visible traction. There is no dominant, adult-focused, character-driven Korean grammar app.

### Cited Findings
- 해커스 앱 is a "기초영문법 어플" for English beginners, teaching must-know grammar through summary notes and video lectures, tied to its "6년 연속 영문법 베스트셀러 1위" book — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/id1617556984)
- 영문법왕국 lets learners "귀여운 캐릭터와 함께" learn grammar (basic grammar mastery) — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=amazingteur.enggrammarkingdom&hl=en_US)
- 영어 문법 - AI 기반: short grammar questions, swipe-based short-video-style UI — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6746693767?l=ko)

## 5. Character/webtoon-based English learning products

## 6. Market size and the price band for a "web app ebook"

### Takeaway
Market-size figures for Korea are old or low-quality. The legacy adult online English players have shrunk sharply (시원스쿨 and 야나두 revenue down about 60 to 80% from 2020 to 2024, unverified), while app subscriptions such as 스픽 lead app revenue. A beginner grammar web-app ebook would naturally price between books (about 11,000 to 13,500원) and app subscriptions (about 99,000 to 129,000원/yr). A one-off price of about 15,000 to 39,000원 is a plausible band (inference, not sourced).

### Cited Findings
